# Privacy Policy — SizeKit

**Last updated:** August 15, 2026

SizeKit (“we”, “the app”) is an offline-first mobile utility that helps you make photos fit upload size and dimension requirements.

## What SizeKit does with your files

- Photos and documents you select are processed **on your device**.
- SizeKit does **not** upload your photos to SizeKit servers.
- SizeKit does **not** require an account.
- Temporary processing files may be created on your device and are cleaned up when possible.

## Permissions

SizeKit may ask for:

- **Photos / media access** — to let you choose an image to process.
- **Save to gallery** — only when you tap Save, so the result can be stored in your gallery.

You can deny permissions; some features will not work until access is granted.

## Advertising (future)

SizeKit may later show ads through Google AdMob to keep the free version available.

When ads are enabled, Google may collect advertising identifiers and device information according to Google’s policies. We will update this policy before live ads ship.

You may be asked for consent for personalized ads in regions that require it (for example, the EU/UK).

A future one-time Pro purchase may remove ads. There is no monthly subscription planned.

## Data we do not collect (today)

At this stage, SizeKit does not operate a backend that stores:

- your photos
- your account profile
- analytics event streams

If that changes, this policy will be updated before those features ship.

## Children’s privacy

SizeKit is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer via the Google Play store listing for SizeKit (once published), or open an issue on the project repository.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
